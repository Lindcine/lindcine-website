02 / THE APPROACH

Find the image
that feels true.

I’m drawn to stories with texture, atmosphere and a strong human core. My approach is collaborative and considered — finding a visual language that feels natural to the subject, while giving every frame a clear sense of intention.

From the first conversation to the final grade, I enjoy being involved in the process of shaping how a story is seen and felt.

03 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Visual by instinct.

I’m Lucas Lindgren, a Stockholm-based cinematographer and filmmaker.

I work across cinematography, camera, lighting and editing — joining productions at different stages, from shaping the visual approach to finding the story in the edit. I enjoy working with people who care about what they make, and projects that leave room for curiosity, craft and collaboration.

Based in Stockholm, Sweden. Available for projects worldwide.
