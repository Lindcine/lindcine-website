A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Visual by instinct.

I'm Lucas Lindgren, a Stockholm-based cinematographer and filmmaker.

I work across cinematography, camera, lighting and editing — from joining larger productions to taking a project from the first idea to the final cut. I enjoy working with different people, stories and creative challenges, always looking for the visual language that serves the story.

Based in Stockholm, Sweden. Available for projects worldwide.
